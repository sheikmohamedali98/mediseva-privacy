# MediSeva privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

MediSeva is a medicine reminder. It does not give medical advice.

## What we store

When you sign in with email or Google, MediSeva stores your email, name, medicines, schedules, and dose history in Google Firebase so they sync across your phones. Crash reports and app analytics are sent to Firebase. Medicine photos stay on your phone and are not included in Android backup.

## What we do not do

MediSeva does not use the advertising ID and does not sell your data.

## Delete your account

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account. That deletes your MediSeva account, your cloud medicines, schedules, and dose history, and clears this device. You must be online. If Google asks you to sign in again, sign in and try once more.

You can also email sheikmohamedali98@gmail.com and ask for deletion. We will delete the Firebase account and cloud data for that login.
